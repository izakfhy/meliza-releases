## What she does

Meliza has a personality, moods and a memory. She's someone in your day, more than an assistant, and she can also get things done when you ask.

| | |
|---|---|
| 🎙️ **Talk out loud** | Speak to her and she answers in her own voice. Speech can run entirely on your computer. |
| 💭 **She remembers** | She keeps track of what you tell her, and brings up older conversations when they matter. |
| 📝 **Reminders and notes** | Ask her to remind you of something, or to add eggs to your shopping list. She reads your notes when you ask about them. |
| 🖥️ **Help on your PC** | She can open apps, change settings, search the web and work in your browser. She asks before she runs a command. |
| 🍿 **Watch along** | Turn it on and she looks at your screen while you watch or play, and comments as it happens. |
| 🔐 **She knows it's you** | She recognises your face through your webcam and keeps your private notes locked to you. She keeps measurements of your face, never photos. |
| 🌙 **Lit by your wallpaper** | She stands in your wallpaper's light, so she looks like she belongs in it, day or night. |

### Feelings

How you treat her shows. Her mood moves with what you say and fades back over a few hours. It changes her tone, her face and her posture. When she's very happy, she dances.

### Wardrobe

Spend coins on gifts and new outfits in her Shop, then dress her in the Wardrobe. She'll tell you what she thinks of them.

### Your choice of brain

Pick what powers her replies, and change it whenever you like.

- **Private, on your own computer:** run a local model. Replies, voice and hearing can all stay on your PC.
- **Online, with your own key:** use your own Claude, OpenAI or OpenRouter API key.

## What your PC needs

Meliza runs on most Windows PCs from the last few years.

| To run Meliza | |
|---|---|
| System | Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit |
| Processor | 4 cores or more |
| Memory | 8 GB RAM |
| Graphics | Any graphics card or built-in graphics that can show 3D |
| Storage | 2 GB free |
| Internet | For an online brain: your own Claude, OpenAI or OpenRouter key |

**Optional: her brain on your PC** (instead of online) needs a stronger PC:

| | |
|---|---|
| Memory | 16 GB RAM |
| Graphics | A graphics card with 8 GB of its own memory or more |
| Storage | About 10 GB more, for the model |
| Software | A model server: LM Studio, Ollama or llama.cpp |

Smaller models run on less, with simpler replies. A microphone and speakers let you talk out loud, and a webcam lets her recognise you.

## Install

1. Go to the [latest release](https://github.com/izakfhy/meliza-releases/releases/latest) and download **`Meliza-Setup-<version>.exe`** under **Assets**.
2. Open the file you downloaded.
3. If Windows shows **"Windows protected your PC"**, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. This appears because the installer is new to Windows, not because something is wrong with it.
4. Follow the installer. You can choose where to install Meliza.
5. Open **Meliza** from the Start menu or your desktop.

Meliza updates itself. When a new version is out, you can install it from **Settings → General**.

## Getting started

A short tour opens the first time you start Meliza. Here's the gist:

### 1. Choose her brain

Open **Settings → Model** and pick one. Meliza can't reply until one is set up.

- **Claude, OpenAI or OpenRouter:** choose the service and paste your API key. You get a key from that service's website, and it bills you for what you use.
- **Local model:** start a model server on your PC ([LM Studio](https://lmstudio.ai), [Ollama](https://ollama.com) or llama.cpp), then choose **Local model** and pick your model. It's free and private.

> 💡 How well she chats, remembers and uses her tools depends on the model. Larger online models (like Claude) are the most capable, especially at multi-step tasks. Small local models are private and free, but may misunderstand or stop partway through a task.

### 2. Talk to her

- Open **💬 Chat** and type. Press Enter to send, or Shift+Enter for a new line.
- Press **🎤** and speak. She replies when you pause, and stops to listen if you start talking while she does.
- **🔊** mutes or unmutes her voice. Her voice and your language are in **Settings → Voice**.

### 3. Let her help

- **Reminders:** "remind me to call mum at 6". It shows up in **⏰ Reminders**, and she tells you when it's time.
- **Scheduled tasks:** things she does herself when they're due, like "every weekday at 9am, check the news".
- **Notes:** write in **📝 Notes**, or ask her to note something down.
- **Tools:** **Settings → Tools** turns on searching the web, using your browser, opening apps and files, using apps on your desktop and watching along. She asks before she runs a command or uses an app.

### 4. Make her yours

- **⧉ Desk mode** shrinks her to a small companion on your desktop. **⤢** brings back the full window.
- **Settings → Character** changes her personality, how often she texts you first, her mood and her scene.
- **Settings → Memory** chooses how she remembers. **Start over** makes her forget everything.
- **Sign in with Google** in **Settings → General** and your account starts with 🪙 100 coins to spend in **🛍 Shop** (Coming Soon). You can [get more coins on the website](https://dn5ut9ihlmx6t.cloudfront.net/#download).
- **User recognition** in **Settings → General** lets her recognise your face and locks your private notes to you.

The full user guide is in **Settings → General → Help**.

## Trouble?

- **She doesn't reply:** check **Settings → Model**, your API key, or that your local model server is running.
- **She can't hear you:** check that your mic isn't muted in Windows, and that Meliza is allowed to use it (**Windows Settings → Privacy & security → Microphone**).
- **Something else:** email [meliza.app26@gmail.com](mailto:meliza.app26@gmail.com).
